How it works

From raw gameplay to an editor-ready first cut.

ShadyGG focuses on the hardest part of editing long gaming content: finding the story and turning it into a useful production plan.

01 · Analysis

Understand the session

The system processes the VOD, transcript, speakers, and available context.

02 · Story ideas

Find the strongest direction

Multiple story candidates are discovered and prepared for human review.

03 · Blueprint

Build one clear story

The approved ideas become one canonical story with a hook, goal, conflict, and payoff.

04 · Director

Create the draft edit

A structured edit plan and Premiere-compatible timeline prepare the project for polishing.
